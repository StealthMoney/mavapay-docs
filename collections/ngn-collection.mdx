---
title: 'Nigerian Naira (NGN) Collections'
description: 'Collect Naira by bank transfer and settle to USDT, BTC, or another currency'
---

Customers pay **Nigerian Naira** by bank transfer. The quote returns a virtual account. They pay that account. Mavapay then credits a wallet or sends the payout.

NGN collection quotes are valid for **10 minutes**.

You can also pay from your **internal NGN wallet** when the balance covers the quote. Pass `sourceWallet: "NGN"` and `skipVirtualAccount: true`, then [accept the quote](/api-reference/endpoint/wallet/accept-quote).

## Where the funds go

| Destination | `targetCurrency` | `autopayout` | `beneficiary` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Internal USDT wallet | `USDTCENT` | `false` | omit |
| External USDT address | `USDTCENT` | `true` | USDT `address` + `network` |
| Internal BTC wallet | `BTCSAT` | `false` | omit |
| External BTC | `BTCSAT` | `true` | Lightning invoice, Lightning address, or on-chain address |
| ZAR bank account | `ZARCENT` | `true` | ZAR bank beneficiary |
| GHS mobile money | `GHSPESEWA` | `true` | GHS MTN beneficiary |
| KES mobile money | `KESCENT` | `true` | KES beneficiary |

`paymentCurrency` is what `amount` means. It must be `sourceCurrency` or `targetCurrency`.

- `NGNKOBO` — kobo (`100` = 1 NGN)
- `USDTCENT` — USDT cents (`10000` = 100 USDT)
- `BTCSAT` — satoshis

`paymentMethod` is `BANKTRANSFER`.

## Buy USDT and send it to an address

The customer pays the virtual account on the quote. USDT is sent to `beneficiary` after the transfer is received.

```bash
curl --request POST \
  --url https://api.mavapay.co/api/v1/quote \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --header 'x-api-key: YOUR_API_KEY' \
  --data '{
    "amount": "10000",
    "sourceCurrency": "NGNKOBO",
    "targetCurrency": "USDTCENT",
    "paymentMethod": "BANKTRANSFER",
    "paymentCurrency": "USDTCENT",
    "autopayout": true,
    "customerReference": "onchain ngn 1",
    "beneficiary": {
      "address": "TR7NHqjeKQxGTCi8q8ZY4pL8ktSzgjLj6t",
      "network": "trc20"
    }
  }'
```

```json
    "exchangeRate": 82002.94497,

    "targetCurrency": "USDTCENT",

    "amountInTargetCurrency": 10000,

```

Pay **exactly** `totalAmountInSourceCurrency` in kobo (`13910500` = ₦139,105.00) to `ngnBankAccountNumber` before `expiry`.

| Field | Use |
|-------|-----|
| `ngnBankAccountNumber` | Account number to pay |
| `ngnAccountName` | Account name |
| `bankName` | Bank |
| `amountInTargetCurrency` | USDT sent, in cents (`10000` = 100 USDT) |

To credit the internal USDT wallet instead, set `autopayout` to `false` and omit `beneficiary`.

## Pay from the internal NGN wallet

Skip the virtual account and debit the NGN wallet.

```json
{
  "amount": "10000",
  "sourceCurrency": "NGNKOBO",
  "targetCurrency": "USDTCENT",
  "paymentMethod": "BANKTRANSFER",
  "paymentCurrency": "USDTCENT",
  "autopayout": true,
  "sourceWallet": "NGN",
  "skipVirtualAccount": true,
  "beneficiary": {
    "address": "TR7NHqjeKQxGTCi8q8ZY4pL8ktSzgjLj6t",
    "network": "trc20"
  }
}
```

```bash
curl --request POST \
  --url https://api.mavapay.co/api/v1/wallet/payout/QUOTE_ID/accept \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --header 'x-api-key: YOUR_API_KEY' \
  --data '{
    "sourceWallet": "NGN"
  }'
```

## Buy BTC

Use `targetCurrency: "BTCSAT"`. `paymentCurrency: "BTCSAT"` means `amount` is satoshis. `paymentCurrency: "NGNKOBO"` means `amount` is kobo.

- Lightning delivery: [Buying Bitcoin (Lightning)](/guides/buying-bitcoin)
- On-chain delivery: [Buying Bitcoin (On-Chain)](/guides/buying-bitcoin-onchain)

`autopayout: false` credits your internal BTC wallet. `autopayout: true` requires a [Bitcoin beneficiary](/guides/beneficiary-formats#bitcoin-btc).

## Other payout currencies

The same NGN payment can settle to another currency. Pass `autopayout: true` and that currency's beneficiary.

- [NGN to ZAR](/guides/ngn-zar)
- [NGN to GHS](/guides/ngn-ghs)
- [NGN to KES](/guides/quote-examples) — `targetCurrency: "KESCENT"`

Beneficiary formats: [USDT](/guides/beneficiary-formats#usdt), [BTC](/guides/beneficiary-formats#bitcoin-btc), [ZAR](/guides/beneficiary-formats#south-african-rand-zar), [KES](/guides/beneficiary-formats#kenyan-shilling-kes), [GHS](/payouts/ghs-payout).
