---
title: 'Crypto Swaps'
description: 'Swap BTC and USDT between your internal Mavapay wallets'
---

Swap BTC into your **USDT wallet**, or USDT into your **BTC wallet**. USDT from a swap is always credited to the internal wallet.

<Warning>
  **Autopayout to an external USDT address is not supported.** Swap into your internal USDT wallet first, then [withdraw USDT](/api-reference/endpoint/withdraw/withdraw-stablecoin) to an external address.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  Swap quotes expire in **60 seconds**. Pay the Lightning invoice or accept the quote before `expiry`, or create a new one.
</Warning>

## How a swap works

USDT always lands in your **internal USDT wallet**. There are two ways to pay for a BTC → USDT swap:

| | Pay Lightning invoice | Debit BTC wallet | USDT → BTC |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sourceCurrency` | `BTCSAT` | `BTCSAT` | `USDTCENT` |
| `targetCurrency` | `USDTCENT` | `USDTCENT` | `BTCSAT` |
| `sourceWallet` | omit | `BTC` | `USDT` |
| How you pay | Pay `invoice` within 60 seconds | [Accept](/api-reference/endpoint/wallet/accept-quote) with `sourceWallet: BTC` | Accept with `sourceWallet: USDT` |
| `paymentMethod` | `LIGHTNING` | `LIGHTNING` | `ONCHAIN` |
| `autopayout` | `false` | `false` | `false` |

`paymentCurrency` controls what `amount` means. If `paymentCurrency` is `USDTCENT`, `amount` is in USDT cents (`1000` = 10 USDT).

## BTC → USDT

USDT is credited to your internal wallet. Autopayout to an external address is not supported.

`amount` is in **USDT cents** because `paymentCurrency` is `USDTCENT`.

### Pay a Lightning invoice

Omit `sourceWallet`. The response includes a Lightning invoice. Pay that invoice within **60 seconds**. Mavapay credits your internal USDT wallet when the payment is received.

```bash
curl --request POST \
  --url https://api.mavapay.co/api/v1/quote \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --header 'x-api-key: YOUR_API_KEY' \
  --data '{
    "amount": "1000",
    "sourceCurrency": "BTCSAT",
    "targetCurrency": "USDTCENT",
    "paymentMethod": "LIGHTNING",
    "paymentCurrency": "USDTCENT",
    "autopayout": false
  }'
```

### Debit your BTC wallet

Pass `sourceWallet: "BTC"`. The response invoice is an internal reference (`MAVAPAY_INTERNAL:...`). Do not pay it. [Accept the quote](#accept-the-swap) to debit your BTC wallet.

```bash
curl --request POST \
  --url https://api.mavapay.co/api/v1/quote \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --header 'x-api-key: YOUR_API_KEY' \
  --data '{
    "amount": "1000",
    "sourceCurrency": "BTCSAT",
    "targetCurrency": "USDTCENT",
    "paymentMethod": "LIGHTNING",
    "paymentCurrency": "USDTCENT",
    "autopayout": false,
    "sourceWallet": "BTC",
    "customerReference": "swap 1"
  }'
```

## USDT → BTC

Debits your USDT wallet and credits your internal BTC wallet.

```bash
curl --request POST \
  --url https://api.mavapay.co/api/v1/quote \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --header 'x-api-key: YOUR_API_KEY' \
  --data '{
    "amount": "1000",
    "sourceCurrency": "USDTCENT",
    "targetCurrency": "BTCSAT",
    "paymentMethod": "ONCHAIN",
    "paymentCurrency": "USDTCENT",
    "autopayout": false,
    "sourceWallet": "USDT",
    "customerReference": "swap 2"
  }'
```

## Accept the swap

Use this only when the quote includes `sourceWallet`. If you requested a Lightning invoice (no `sourceWallet`), pay `invoice` instead.

Call [Accept Quote](/api-reference/endpoint/wallet/accept-quote) with the quote `id` and the wallet to debit. This must match `sourceWallet` on the quote.

**BTC → USDT** — debit BTC:

```bash
curl --request POST \
  --url https://api.mavapay.co/api/v1/wallet/payout/a98d4e1d-9239-49e4-b4a2-67bd20f335e0/accept \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --header 'x-api-key: YOUR_API_KEY' \
  --data '{
    "sourceWallet": "BTC"
  }'
```

**USDT → BTC** — debit USDT:

```bash
curl --request POST \
  --url https://api.mavapay.co/api/v1/wallet/payout/4a4467fd-c39d-46b0-bd90-23af5aca90bd/accept \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --header 'x-api-key: YOUR_API_KEY' \
  --data '{
    "sourceWallet": "USDT"
  }'
```

The wallet must cover `totalAmountInSourceCurrency` (includes fees). If the quote has expired, create a new one.

## After a BTC → USDT swap

USDT lands in your internal wallet. To send it on-chain:

1. Confirm the USDT balance with [Get wallets](/api-reference/endpoint/wallet/get-wallets) (`currency=USDT`)
2. [Withdraw](/api-reference/endpoint/withdraw/withdraw-stablecoin) to an external address
