Buy USDT with Naira. You can send the USDT to an external address, or credit your internal USDT wallet.

NGN → USDT quotes are valid for **10 minutes**.

## Choose how you pay and where USDT goes

| You want to… | Set these fields |
|---|---|
| Customer pays from an external NGN bank account | Omit `sourceWallet`. Pay the virtual account on the quote. |
| Pay from your internal NGN wallet | `"sourceWallet": "NGN"` and `"skipVirtualAccount": true`, then [accept the quote](/api-reference/endpoint/wallet/accept-quote) |
| Send USDT to an external address | `"autopayout": true` and a `beneficiary` with `address` + `network` |
| Credit your internal USDT wallet | `"autopayout": false` (no beneficiary) |

`paymentCurrency` controls what `amount` means. With `"paymentCurrency": "USDTCENT"`, `"10000"` is **100 USDT**.

## Pay from an external bank account

USDT is sent to the beneficiary address after the NGN transfer is received.

```bash
curl --request POST \
  --url https://api.mavapay.co/api/v1/quote \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --header 'x-api-key: YOUR_API_KEY' \
  --data '{
    "amount": "10000",
    "sourceCurrency": "NGNKOBO",
    "targetCurrency": "USDTCENT",
    "paymentMethod": "BANKTRANSFER",
    "paymentCurrency": "USDTCENT",
    "autopayout": true,
    "customerReference": "onchain ngn 1",
    "beneficiary": {
      "address": "TR7NHqjeKQxGTCi8q8ZY4pL8ktSzgjLj6t",
      "network": "trc20"
    }
  }'
```

Pay **exactly** `totalAmountInSourceCurrency` (kobo) to the virtual account:

| Field | Use |
|-------|-----|
| `ngnBankAccountNumber` | Account number to pay |
| `ngnAccountName` | Account name |
| `bankName` | Bank |
| `totalAmountInSourceCurrency` | Amount in kobo (`13910500` = ₦139,105.00) |
| `expiry` | Quote is valid for 10 minutes |

When payment is received you get `payment.received`, then `payment.sent` when USDT is sent to the beneficiary.

## Pay from your internal NGN wallet

If your NGN wallet has enough balance, skip the virtual account and accept the quote.

```bash
curl --request POST \
  --url https://api.mavapay.co/api/v1/quote \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --header 'x-api-key: YOUR_API_KEY' \
  --data '{
    "amount": "10000",
    "sourceCurrency": "NGNKOBO",
    "targetCurrency": "USDTCENT",
    "paymentMethod": "BANKTRANSFER",
    "paymentCurrency": "USDTCENT",
    "autopayout": true,
    "sourceWallet": "NGN",
    "skipVirtualAccount": true,
    "customerReference": "internal ngn usdt",
    "beneficiary": {
      "address": "TR7NHqjeKQxGTCi8q8ZY4pL8ktSzgjLj6t",
      "network": "trc20"
    }
  }'
```

Then accept with `sourceWallet: "NGN"`:

```bash
curl --request POST \
  --url https://api.mavapay.co/api/v1/wallet/payout/c4b76db5-c9f8-452d-b40b-4c1a065190bf/accept \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --header 'x-api-key: YOUR_API_KEY' \
  --data '{
    "sourceWallet": "NGN"
  }'
```

Your NGN wallet is debited immediately. No bank transfer is required.

## Credit your internal USDT wallet

Set `autopayout` to `false` and omit `beneficiary`. USDT is credited to your Mavapay USDT wallet instead of an external address.

```json
{
  "amount": "10000",
  "sourceCurrency": "NGNKOBO",
  "targetCurrency": "USDTCENT",
  "paymentMethod": "BANKTRANSFER",
  "paymentCurrency": "USDTCENT",
  "autopayout": false,
  "customerReference": "usdt to wallet"
}
```

Pay the virtual account (external NGN) or pass `sourceWallet: "NGN"` + `skipVirtualAccount: true` and accept (internal NGN).

## Beneficiary

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| `address` | Yes, when `autopayout` is `true` | Destination USDT address |
| `network` | Yes, when `autopayout` is `true` | `trc20`, `erc20`, `plasma`, `solana`, or `polygon` |

The address must match the network. See [USDT networks](/stablecoins/introduction#supported-networks).
